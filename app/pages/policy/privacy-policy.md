# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 31, 2026

Welcome to PETER.

PETER is an attendance management system designed to help organizations manage employee or member attendance through streamlined clock-in and clock-out workflows, attendance records, and face-based verification.

This Privacy Policy explains how PETER ("PETER", "we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the PETER website, applications, demonstrations, and related services.

PETER is designed to provide organizations with attendance-management capabilities while following a privacy-conscious approach to handling personal information.

"PETER" is the name of the software system and project described in this Privacy Policy; it is not itself a separate legal entity. The Service is developed, owned, and operated by **Isaac Yancy Paala**, operating under the developer name **yncynotdev**. Throughout this Privacy Policy, references to "PETER," "we," "us," and "our" refer to **Isaac Yancy Paala (yncynotdev)** as the developer and operator of the Service, unless the context clearly refers only to the software system itself.

## 1. About PETER

PETER is an actively developed software system intended to provide practical attendance solutions for organizations.

PETER is not merely a personal or experimental project. It is designed and developed with the intention of serving real organizations and their attendance-management needs. Features, infrastructure, integrations, and supported deployment environments may continue to evolve as the system is developed and deployed for different organizations.

PETER may be provided as a hosted service, organization-specific deployment, demonstration environment, or other deployment arrangement depending on the organization's requirements.

## 2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how PETER is deployed and used, the system may process information such as:

* Name
* Employee or member identification information
* Attendance dates and times
* Clock-in and clock-out records
* Attendance status
* Organization or department information
* Account information
* Device or browser information
* Technical logs necessary for operating and securing the Service
* Images captured during attendance verification, depending on the deployment configuration

The exact information collected may differ between organizations and deployment environments.

## 3. Face Detection and Biometric Information

PETER may use face detection as part of its attendance-verification process.

PETER is designed so that biometric data is not stored as a permanent biometric database for attendance verification.

A camera image may be temporarily processed or transmitted depending on the deployment configuration in order to perform the attendance workflow. The precise handling of images may therefore vary between deployments.

Organizations using PETER are responsible for determining the appropriate legal basis, notices, consent requirements, retention periods, and other privacy obligations applicable to their use of face-based attendance verification.

PETER should not be understood as providing legal advice regarding biometric or privacy compliance.

## 4. Attendance Information

Attendance information may include:

* The identity of the person recording attendance
* Date and time of attendance
* Clock-in or clock-out status
* Verification status
* Organization-related information
* Other information required to operate the attendance workflow

This information is generally processed to provide attendance-management functionality to the organization using PETER.

The organization operating a PETER deployment may determine how attendance information is used, retained, reviewed, and managed.

## 5. How Information Is Used

Information processed through PETER may be used to:

* Record attendance
* Verify attendance attempts
* Reduce attendance fraud such as buddy-punching
* Manage employee or member attendance
* Provide organization-specific workflows
* Maintain and improve the Service
* Diagnose technical problems
* Protect the Service against abuse or unauthorized access
* Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not intend to use attendance information for purposes unrelated to providing or supporting the Service unless otherwise disclosed or legally required.

## 6. Data Storage and Infrastructure

PETER's storage architecture depends on the deployment configuration.

PETER is currently primarily designed around Cloudflare-based infrastructure, which may include services such as Cloudflare Workers, Cloudflare Pages, Cloudflare R2, and related Cloudflare infrastructure.

Certain deployments may also use external databases or other infrastructure depending on the organization's requirements.

Because PETER is actively developed, the technologies and infrastructure used to operate the Service may change over time.

The location and processing of information may therefore depend on the infrastructure selected for a particular deployment.

Organizations should not use PETER to process information relating to children or minors unless they have established an appropriate lawful basis and safeguards for doing so.

If a deployment is specifically intended for minors, additional privacy and data-protection requirements may apply.
